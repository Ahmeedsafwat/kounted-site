02 / The problem

Finding creators and reporting on them both eat the week.

  1. Creators picked on follower count.

    Shortlists get built by scrolling profiles for days. A follower count says little about what a creator's posts actually do today.

  2. Numbers frozen on the day someone checked.

    A manual report records each post once. Views that arrive in week two or three never make it into the report, so creators look weaker than they were.

  3. Saves and shares stay hidden.

    On Instagram those numbers sit in each creator's own insights. Asking 40 creators for screenshots takes days, and some never reply.

  4. Cross-posts get counted twice.

    A collab Reel appears on two profiles. Add both and the campaign looks bigger than it was, until someone checks.

  5. Platforms you only half use.

    An annual contract, seats for the whole team, and a dashboard that someone exports to a spreadsheet anyway.

03 / Sourcing and vetting

We pick creators on what their posts do, not on follower count.

Send a brief with your niche, markets, platform, follower range and content style. We build a shortlist that matches it, then vet every name on real post data.

How we vet each creator

  1. Median views of the last 12 posts.

    Pinned posts and posts under 7 days old are left out, so one old hit or a post still climbing does not skew it.

  2. Engagement rate measured on views.

    Likes, comments, saves and shares divided by views. Not by followers, which most audiences never see.

  3. Posting consistency.

    How often they post, and whether that has held over recent months.

  4. Engagement authenticity check.

    It flags posts for review when the engagement shows patterns often seen with bought likes or views. A flag means a person looks closer. It is not a verdict.

  5. Niche and brand fit notes.

    A short written note on each creator's content, tone and audience fit against your brief.

Checked against your list

We compare every candidate with your existing creator list, so you never pay for names you already have.

What only the creator can show

Audience demographics and story views are private. We mark them clearly and give you a ready-made request to send the creator, instead of guessing.

Public emails only

Contact details come only from emails a creator lists publicly.

One round of replacements

If a creator does not fit, we replace them once. The shortlist lands in your own Google Sheet.

09 / Questions

Short answers.

Do you only use public data?

Yes, for both sourcing and measurement. We never ask for passwords, and creators do not need to grant access or install anything. Anything only a creator can see, such as audience demographics, we mark clearly and leave for the creator to share.

Which platforms do you cover?

Instagram and TikTok. On Instagram that means feed posts, carousels and Reels. If you run campaigns somewhere else, ask. We will tell you plainly if we cannot cover it.

How do you find creators?

We start from your brief. We search by niche, by market and through accounts similar to ones that fit, then vet each candidate on public post data.

Can you check creators we already found?

Yes. Send the handles and we vet them the same way, on median views, engagement on views, consistency, the authenticity check and fit.

Where do creator emails come from?

Only from what creators publish themselves, such as the email in their profile. We do not buy or guess contact details.

How is the Google Sheet shared?

The Sheet sits in your Google account and you add us as an editor. You own it, you decide who sees it, and it stays with you if we stop working together.

How is an engagement scoped?

For measurement, by the number of markets you run in and the number of posts you track. For sourcing, by the number of creators you need. We usually quote after a free audit or shortlist, in writing. There are no tiers and no seats.

What are the contract terms?

Month to month. No annual contract and no seat licences. If you stop, you keep the Sheet and its full history.

Do you replace our influencer platform?

Partly. We do creator sourcing, vetting and measurement. We do not run a creator CRM or payments. If you need those, keep your platform for them and use Kounted alongside it.
